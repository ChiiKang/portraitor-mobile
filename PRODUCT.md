# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People using exported conversations to understand the communication patterns, tone, and personality of someone in their life. Onboarding serves first-time users who need a clear explanation and confidence about privacy before starting an analysis.

## Product Purpose

Portraitor turns conversation exports into thoughtful personality portraits. The mobile experience should make starting an analysis feel simple, safe, and emotionally approachable while preserving user control over private chat data.

## Brand Personality

Calm, intimate, and trustworthy. Warm visual storytelling should make the AI analysis feel human without becoming clinical, mystical, or overstated.

## Anti-references

Avoid clinical health-app styling, cold corporate dashboards, generic SaaS onboarding, noisy gamification, and visual changes that depart from the approved Portraitor handover.

## Design Principles

- Match approved handover details before introducing new visual interpretation.
- Explain the workflow in plain language before asking for action.
- Make privacy visible, specific, and reassuring.
- Keep interaction familiar and native while retaining Portraitor's warmth.
- Use visual richness to support trust and comprehension, not decoration alone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve native SafeArea behavior and real system chrome. Provide semantic labels, sufficient touch targets, readable contrast, reduced-motion behavior, and resilient text layout without building mock phone chrome into the app.
